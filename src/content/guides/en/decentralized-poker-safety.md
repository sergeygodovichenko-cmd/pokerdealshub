# How Not to Lose Money in Decentralized Poker Apps with Club Systems

In decentralized poker apps, mistakes are costly. Understanding how the system works helps players not lose money.

## What Are Decentralized Poker Apps

In apps like PPPoker, ClubGG, and X-Poker, clubs are created by poker enthusiasts. Several clubs unite into unions, creating a unified player pool.

## Advantages

- **Softer games** - Club owners are interested in retaining weak players
- **High rakeback** - More opportunities for profitable play

## Reputation Decides Everything

Large unions with long history have turned into a business where reputation is the main asset. Losing a player or tarnishing the name means undermining their existence.
